

## **EPIC**
An **epic** in Agile is ==a large body of work that is too big to finish in a single sprint and must be broken down into smaller, manageable pieces called **user stories**==.

## **SPIKE**
**spike in Agile** is ==a **time-boxed research or exploration activity** used to investigate technical or functional unknowns, evaluate solutions, or reduce risk before committing to full product development==. 

## **OPS**
**Ops (Operations)** in the context of Agile methodology refers to ==the application of Agile principles—such as iterative delivery, cross-functional collaboration, and continuous feedback—to IT operations, system maintenance, deployment pipelines, and infrastructure management==.

## **CHORE**
==a purely technical task that does not directly deliver new business value or features to the end-user, but is necessary to keep the project, repository, or infrastructure running smoothly==.
